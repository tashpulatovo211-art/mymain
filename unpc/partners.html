Partners

UNPC is free for students. These organizations give us our cases, our prizes and a place to hold the tournament.

    Become a partner

    We hold a tournament every Sunday, so you can support a single Sunday or every week.

    What partners get

    • Your logo on this website
    • Your logo on our banners and certificates
    • Posts about you on our Telegram channel
    • Thanks from the stage at every award ceremony

    What helps most

    • Prizes for winning teams, such as subscriptions or vouchers
    • Drinks and snacks for the coffee break
    • A hall for one Sunday tournament
    • University teachers from any field who can judge a room
    • Guest speakers for a 30-minute talk, online or in person
