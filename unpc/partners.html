What helps most

  • Prizes for winning teams, such as subscriptions or vouchers

    Awards Shows up during: the award ceremony.

  • Drinks and snacks for the coffee break

    Preparation Shows up during: preparation.

  • A hall for one Sunday tournament

    The whole day Shows up during: the whole day, from the welcome to the award ceremony.

  • University teachers from any field to judge a room

    Presenting and questions Shows up during: presentations and questions.

  • Guest speakers to talk for 30 minutes, online or in person

    Welcome Shows up during: the welcome and guest speaker.

What partners get

  • Your logo on the website.
  • Your logo on banners and certificates.
  • Posts on the UNPC Telegram channel.
  • Thanks from the stage at every award ceremony.
